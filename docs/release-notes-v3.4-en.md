Release v3.4 — August 17, 2026

What's new and what's fixed on igovazd.am since v3.3.

— Pictures —
1. A picture that is too small for the field it goes into is now refused right away, in red, naming both the size that is required and the size you picked. Before this, a small photo was accepted silently and stored at whatever size it came in at — the site never enlarges a picture — so a poster dropped at 640×360 went out to the catalog looking blurry, under a hint that said 1600×900 was expected.
2. The rule follows the field: 1600×900 for posters, stills and galleries, 800×800 for cast and crew photos, 512×512 for avatars and logos. A bigger picture is still welcome — the crop step frames it and the site stores it at exactly the size above.
3. Because a picture that fails the check is never attached, a project can no longer be published or sent for review with an undersized image in it.

— Advertising filters —
4. In the advertising section, the "Platform" filter for video channels no longer collides with the "Available on" filter that lists where a film can be watched. The two shared one name behind the scenes, so ticking a box in one could disturb the other and the address bar could not tell them apart.
