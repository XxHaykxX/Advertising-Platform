Release v3.1 — August 11, 2026

What's new and what's fixed on igovazd.am since v3.0.

— Your account —
1. One account can now be both sides of the marketplace. Selling your own project and buying
   placement in someone else's used to need two separate sign-ups with two different email
   addresses. There is now a switch under your avatar: turn on the other side and it is
   available immediately, with the same login. What you can do follows what you switched on,
   not what you picked the day you registered.
2. You cannot apply to your own listing, or save it to favourites. Before, a creator who also
   had a brand account could send themselves an application. The button and the heart are now
   simply absent on anything you own.
3. Signing in as a member no longer signs you out of the staff panel, and the other way round.

— Advertising space —
4. Advertising space outside a film is now for sale. Billboards, lift posters, transit,
   radio, TV, video and banner inventory get their own listings, with photos, location,
   size or format, daily reach and availability dates. A creator adds them in "My ad
   spaces"; they go through the same review queue as a project and appear on their
   channel's page once approved.
5. City is picked from a list instead of typed. Typing it by hand meant the same city
   arrived spelled three ways and stopped matching the filter. The list covers 20 cities and
   still lets you enter one that isn't there.

— The catalog —
6. The catalog shows every kind of advertising in one place — projects and ad space
   together — and the filters are built from what is actually listed, so you never pick a
   filter that returns nothing.
7. Result counts are grammatical in Russian and Armenian. "2 рекламных мест" is now
   "2 рекламных места"; a search that finds nothing no longer leaves a dangling "Showing"
   above the empty result.
8. The platform filter printed one entry in Armenian to Russian and English readers. Fixed
   in the listings themselves, so it reads correctly in all three languages.
9. Countries are shown in your language everywhere, and one project's country list carried
   an entry that had no meaning outside Armenian; it has been removed.

— Navigation —
10. A brand no longer loses the whole menu when leaving the buyer cabinet. Opening the
    catalog, a project page or the advertising section left the top bar empty; the two
    cabinet links now follow you everywhere except the creator side.
11. The advertising section is reachable from a dropdown in the header, all nine channels
    at once, and creators have a guide linked from the footer.

— Submitting a project —
12. The form no longer offers two differently-named ways out of the same page. The link at
    the top says where it takes you — back to your cabinet — and the button in the toolbar
    says what happens to your work: on a new project it saves the draft before leaving, and
    it now really does save it rather than relying on the autosave timer.
13. Messages on the ad-space form talked about "the project". Submitting a billboard and
    being told a project can't be submitted was confusing; every message on that side now
    says what it means. One of them was outright wrong: a permission refusal was showing
    "fill in the title, synopsis and at least one genre".

— Notifications —
14. If your browser held both a staff session and a member session, the member cabinet
    showed the staff inbox — someone else's notifications, with "mark all as read" acting on
    them. Each cabinet now reads only its own. Push subscriptions were registering under the
    wrong account for the same reason and are fixed with it.
