Release v3.5 — August 19, 2026

What's new and what's fixed on igovazd.am since v3.4.

— Advertising, restructured —
1. The catalogue now opens on four groups instead of nine channels: outdoor, digital, broadcast, and sponsorship with product placement. A group page shows the inventory of every channel under it at once and adds a "Channel" filter to narrow it down.
2. `/ads` is a redirect rather than a page. With a channel in the address it opens that channel; without one it goes to the four cards on the home page. `/catalog` goes to the same place. The single everything-at-once list is gone — someone buying a billboard was scrolling past films and radio to find one.
3. The marketing pages that used to sit in front of each channel are gone too. A channel now leads straight to what it has for sale.

— A home page that sells —
4. The home page was rebuilt to the brief: what the site is for, the four ways to advertise, why here, partner logos, client video, the advertiser's seven goals, and a way to get in touch.
5. Seven goals — brand awareness, traffic, leads, sales support, warming an audience, app promotion, key accounts — each opening the group of channels that serves it. They are worded for what this inventory genuinely does towards a goal: a billboard carries no pixel and no store attribution, so nothing here promises measurement it cannot deliver.
6. Sections that left the home page all kept their own: "how it works" at /how-it-works, the FAQ at /faq, the case studies at /portfolio.

— Client video —
7. A carousel of client clips, added by staff. Previews play silently on a loop rather than showing a frozen frame — a still image reads as a picture, a moving one reads as video. Pressing play opens it full size with sound.
8. The carousel takes its proportions from the clip itself instead of forcing 16:9. Clients send what their phone or their agency shot, and a vertical video squeezed into a wide frame left a horizontal slice of whoever was speaking.
9. Every text field on a testimonial is optional and nothing renders empty. A clip on its own is enough; the quote and the name are what staff adds when there is something to add.
10. Video uploads no longer have a size limit. The old 50 MB ceiling was chosen for shared hosting, and a phone shooting in 4K passes it in about a minute.

— Getting in touch —
11. There is one request form now, on the contact page, and the home page's second button opens it. It asks for a phone number rather than an email address, and the notification that reaches us dials it back.
12. The messages around that form no longer promise a reply by email. One of them said to check your inbox for a confirmation, which was never sent.

— Fixed —
13. On a phone, the menu opened, looked correct, and ignored every tap on its own links — only the close button responded. The dimming behind the panel was being drawn on top of the panel itself and swallowing the taps.
14. Restoring an earlier version of a case study, a partner or a person from the change history silently did nothing. The three sections were missing a field the restore needed.
15. A filter that belonged to a different advertising section could empty a page's results while never appearing on it, leaving a page that looked unfiltered and showed nothing.
